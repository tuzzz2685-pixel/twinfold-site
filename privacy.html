Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Developer: Yufei Wang · 中文

Twinfold asks you one question a day and keeps your answers as a personal archive. What you write is yours. We built the app so that we never receive it.

We don't collect your archive

Twinfold has no accounts, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app doesn't send your answers, notes, photos, recordings, or anything else in your archive to us or to any third party. We can't see anything you write there. The only thing that ever reaches us from the app is feedback you choose to send (see below).

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores these on your device:

  • Your answers, notes, and later updates to answers, with the date and the question as you saw it
  • Photos and voice recordings you attach, and the text transcribed from recordings
  • Your settings, such as reminder time and whether the app is locked
  • Whether you've bought the full version

The home screen widget reads today's question and whether you've answered it from the same device.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Twinfold syncs your archive through your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices. That storage belongs to your Apple Account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Recordings and transcription

Recordings are turned into text by Apple's speech recognition running on your iPhone. Twinfold only uses on-device recognition, so your voice isn't sent anywhere to be transcribed. You can choose in Settings whether to keep the original recordings after they've been transcribed.

Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: only when you record an answer.
  • Photos: no access to your photo library is needed. The iOS photo picker hands the app only the photos you choose to attach.
  • Notifications: daily reminders, scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: if you turn on the lock. iOS checks your face or fingerprint; the app only learns whether it matched.

When you export or share

You can export your archive as a Markdown or JSON file, make a backup file, or share a question card or star map image. These leave the app only when you choose where to send them through the iOS share sheet. Once you send your archive to an AI service or another app, what happens to it is governed by that company's privacy policy, not ours.

Feedback you send us

Settings has a Send feedback page. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and only these go out:

  • The message you write on that page
  • Your email address, only if you enter one so we can reply
  • The app version and iOS version, to help us track down problems

Nothing from your archive is included. The form is delivered through Formspree, a form service that forwards it to our email inbox. We use feedback only to answer you and improve the app, never for advertising or tracking, and we don't link it to anything else about you. To have your feedback deleted, email us. You can also skip the form and email us directly.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Crash reports

If you've chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They don't include your answers or anything you've written. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

You can delete any answer, update, or note inside the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To remove the synced copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, choose Twinfold, and delete its data. We hold none of your archive, so there's nothing of it for us to delete on our side. If you've sent us feedback, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights (including GDPR)

Yufei Wang is the developer of Twinfold. We don't collect or process your archive: everything the app stores is under your control, and you can see it, export it, correct it, and delete it yourself at any time. The only personal data we receive is feedback you choose to send, which we process with your consent to reply to you; you can ask us to see or delete it at any time. If you have a question about your privacy, email us. If you're in the EU or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Twinfold collects no personal information from anyone, including children, apart from feedback someone chooses to send us.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we'll update this page with a new effective date before that version is released.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日 · 开发者：Yufei Wang

Twinfold 每天问你一个问题，把你的回答攒成一份个人档案。你写下的内容属于你，我们在设计上就让自己收不到它。

我们不收集你的档案

Twinfold 没有账号，没有我们自己的服务器，没有广告、统计或追踪。App 不会把你的回答、备忘、照片、录音或档案里的任何其他内容发给我们或任何第三方，你在档案里写的内容我们都看不到。App 里唯一会到我们手上的，是你主动发来的反馈（见下文）。

保存在你 iPhone 上的内容

  • 你的回答、备忘和对回答的更新，连同日期和你当时看到的题面
  • 你附上的照片和录音，以及录音转写出的文字
  • 你的设置，比如提醒时间、是否开启锁
  • 是否已购买完整版

桌面小组件在同一台设备上读取今日题目和你是否已答。

iCloud

如果你登录了 iCloud，Twinfold 会通过你自己的私有 iCloud 数据库同步档案，让它出现在你的其他设备上。这部分存储属于你的 Apple 账户，由 Apple 按照 Apple 隐私政策处理，我们无法访问。

录音和转写

录音由 iPhone 上 Apple 的语音识别转成文字。Twinfold 只使用设备上的离线识别，你的声音不会被发往任何地方转写。转写之后要不要保留原始录音，可以在设置里选。

权限

  • 麦克风和语音识别：只在你录音回答时使用。
  • 照片：不需要相册权限。系统的照片选择器只把你选中的照片交给 App。
  • 通知：每日提醒，在你的 iPhone 上本地安排。
  • 面容 ID / 触控 ID：开启锁时使用。由 iOS 识别，App 只知道是否匹配。

导出和分享

你可以把档案导出成 Markdown 或 JSON 文件、做备份文件，或分享题卡、星图图片。只有你通过系统分享面板选择发往哪里时，它们才会离开 App。档案一旦发给某个 AI 服务或其他 App，就适用那家公司的隐私政策，而不是本政策。

你发给我们的反馈

设置里有“发送反馈”页面。点“发送”才会发出，发出的只有：

  • 你在这一页写的内容
  • 你的邮箱，只在你为了收到回复而填写时才发
  • App 版本和 iOS 版本，方便我们排查问题

不带档案里的任何内容。表单经由表单服务 Formspree 转发到我们的邮箱。反馈只用来回复你和改进 App，不用于广告或追踪，也不和你的其他信息关联。想删除你发过的反馈，发邮件告诉我们即可。你也可以不用表单，直接给我们写邮件。

购买

完整版是一次性购买，由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们收不到你的支付信息。

崩溃报告

如果你在 iOS 里选择了与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会把匿名的崩溃报告和使用统计转给我们，其中不包含你的回答或你写的任何内容。可以在“设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进”里更改。

删除你的数据

你可以在 App 里删除任何一条回答、更新或备忘。删除 App 会清除 iPhone 上存的全部内容。要删除同步的那份，前往“设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理账户储存空间”，选择 Twinfold 并删除其数据。我们不持有你的档案，所以这部分我们这边没有需要删除的。如果你给我们发过反馈，发邮件告诉我们，我们会删掉。

你的权利（包括 GDPR）

Twinfold 的开发者是 Yufei Wang。我们不收集也不处理你的档案，App 存下的一切都由你掌控：你随时可以自己查看、导出、更正和删除。我们收到的个人数据只有你主动发来的反馈，基于你的同意用来回复你；你随时可以要求查看或删除。有隐私方面的问题请发邮件给我们。如果你在欧盟或英国，你也有权向当地的数据保护机构投诉。

儿童

除了用户主动发来的反馈，Twinfold 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果以后的版本改变了数据处理方式，我们会在该版本发布前更新本页并注明新的生效日期。
