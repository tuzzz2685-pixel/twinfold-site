Questions, problems, or ideas: email tuzzz2685@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version. We read every message.

How do I give my archive to an AI?

On the Archive tab, tap “Give it to your AI” and pick the Markdown file. The iOS share sheet lets you send it to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other app, or save it to Files and add it to a project in your AI. The file starts with a note telling the AI how to use it. When you send a newer one to a project, delete the old file there first, since the AI services keep both copies.

I got a new iPhone. Where are my answers?

If both phones use the same Apple Account with iCloud on, your archive syncs by itself. It can take a few minutes on first launch. You can also go to Settings › Back up and restore, save a backup file, and restore it on the new phone. Restoring only adds what's missing; it never overwrites answers you already have.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings › Unlock all questions and tap “Restore purchase”. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My recording wasn't turned into text.

Twinfold transcribes on your iPhone only, so your voice never leaves it. That needs iOS on-device speech recognition for your language. If your iPhone doesn't support it, or a recording is very long, part of it may not be transcribed. You can type the text into the answer yourself.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications for Twinfold are allowed in the iOS Settings app, and that the daily reminder is on in Twinfold's settings.

Face ID didn't recognize me.

Use your iPhone passcode instead. You can turn the lock off in Twinfold's settings.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete single answers, updates, and notes in the app. To delete everything, delete the app, then remove its iCloud data in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Twinfold. See the Privacy Policy for details.

支持

有问题、遇到故障或有想法，请发邮件到 tuzzz2685@gmail.com，最好写上你的 iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本。每封邮件我们都会看。

怎么把档案交给 AI？

在“档案”页点“交给你的 AI”，选 Markdown 文件，通过系统分享面板发给 ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini 或其他 App，也可以存到“文件”里再放进 AI 的项目。文件开头有一段写给 AI 的说明，告诉它怎么用。往项目里放新的一份时，先删掉旧文件，各家都会把两份都留着。

换了新 iPhone，回答在哪？

两台手机用同一个 Apple 账户并开着 iCloud 时，档案会自动同步，第一次打开可能要等几分钟。也可以在“设置 › 备份与恢复”里存一个备份文件，到新手机上恢复。恢复只补上缺的，不会覆盖已有的回答。

怎么恢复购买？

到“设置 › 解锁完整题库”，点“恢复购买”，用当初购买时的 Apple 账户。

录音没有转成文字。

Twinfold 只在 iPhone 上转写，声音不会离开手机，这需要 iOS 支持你所用语言的离线语音识别。手机不支持或录音很长时，可能有一部分没转出来，可以自己把文字补进回答里。

收不到提醒。

检查 iOS“设置”里是否允许 Twinfold 发通知，以及 Twinfold 设置里的每日提醒是否开着。

面容 ID 认不出我。

改用 iPhone 密码解锁。也可以在 Twinfold 的设置里关掉锁。

怎么删除我的数据？

在 App 里可以删除单条回答、更新和备忘。要全部删除，就删掉 App，再到“设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理账户储存空间 › Twinfold”删除它的 iCloud 数据。详见隐私政策。
