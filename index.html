One Question a Day · iPhone

Answer one question a day, by typing or just talking. Each answer adds to an archive of who you are: what you've lived through, what you value, the people in your life, what you hope for.

Export the whole archive as a Markdown file anytime and give it to the AI you use, so it understands you better.

Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. No account, no servers of ours. We can't see anything you write.
